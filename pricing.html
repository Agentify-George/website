Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about AVA — Answered

Can I create unlimited AI voice agents?
Yes, you can create as many agents as you need. Our platform supports unlimited agent creation across all paid plans to suit various business needs.
How does the SMS Add-on work?
The SMS add-on allows your agents to send follow-up texts. You purchase credits that are used per message sent, giving you flexibility based on volume.
What's included in my AI calling minutes?
Minutes include both inbound and outbound conversation time. System processing time is not charged, only the actual duration of the call.
Do I need a Twilio account?
No, we handle the telephony infrastructure. You can port existing numbers or purchase new ones directly through our dashboard.
Can I use Thinkrr with my CRM and calendar?
Yes, we have native integrations with major CRMs and Calendar providers (Google, Outlook) to sync appointments and contacts automatically.
What CRM's do you integrate with?
We integrate with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive, and offer Zapier/Make connectivity for thousands of other tools.
