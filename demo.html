Have Other Questions, We're Here to Help.

Thoughtful answers to common questions about how Agentify operates.

Is this prerecorded or scripted?

No. This is a live AI voice agent. Megan generates responses in real time based on what you say—there are no recordings, no scripts, and no fixed paths.

Is Megan replacing human staff?

No. She handles the repetitive front-line work—answering calls, gathering context, and routing intent—so humans can focus on conversations that actually require judgment and nuance.

What kinds of calls can Megan handle?

Megan is designed for common inbound scenarios like answering questions, booking appointments, qualifying leads, and routing callers appropriately. If a situation needs a human, she knows when to step aside.

Is my voice or personal data stored?

No personal voice data is stored from this demo. This experience is designed to be lightweight, private, and safe to explore without committing information.

What happens if I want something like this for my business?

If the experience feels useful, the next step is a short discovery call. We’ll assess your call volume, workflows, and goals—and determine whether an AI voice agent actually makes sense for you.
