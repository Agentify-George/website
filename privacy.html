Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 01/21/2026

Agentify (“Agentify,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you access or use our website, software, applications, AI agents, voice systems, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

By using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and business information
  • Account credentials and profile information
  • Payment and billing details (processed via third-party payment providers)
  • Communications with us (emails, support requests, feedback)

b. Information Processed Through AI, Voice & Messaging Systems

When you use Agentify’s automation, voice, or messaging features, we may process:

  • Call metadata (date, time, duration, phone numbers)
  • Message content and interaction data
  • Transcriptions, summaries, or AI-generated outputs
  • Configuration data and workflows you create

Important: Agentify processes this data solely to provide and improve the Services and does not independently contact end users.

c. Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

  • IP address, browser type, device information
  • Usage data, logs, and performance metrics
  • Cookies or similar technologies for functionality and analytics

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services
  • Process transactions and manage accounts
  • Power AI agents, automation, and workflows
  • Improve performance, reliability, and user experience
  • Provide customer support and communicate with you
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

3. AI & Automated Processing Disclosure

Agentify uses artificial intelligence and automated systems to process data and generate outputs based on user configuration.

You acknowledge that:

  • AI outputs are probabilistic and may not always be accurate

  • You control how AI agents are configured and deployed

Agentify does not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about individuals without user direction.

If you use voice, SMS, or messaging features:
